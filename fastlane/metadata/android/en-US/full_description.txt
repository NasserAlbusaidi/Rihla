Rihla is the easiest way to split bills and track group expenses with friends, family, and roommates — and see exactly who owes who, across every trip you take together.

Most bill splitters forget your group the moment a trip ends. Rihla keeps a permanent group for your people, so balances carry over from one trip to the next. One tap shows who owes whom across every outing your group has ever shared — no more starting from zero.

WHY RIHLA

• Persistent groups, not one-off trips. Keep a group for your friends and start a new trip, dinner, or outing inside it anytime. Balances add up across all of them.
• Settle up with the fewest payments. Rihla works out the simplest set of transfers to clear everyone's debts — for a single trip or across the whole group.
• Split it any way you like. Share an expense evenly, by weighted shares, by exact amounts, or by percentage — across everyone, a chosen few, or just yourself.
• Works fully offline. Add expenses and settle up with no signal — everything syncs automatically the moment you're back online.
• Start in seconds. No account, no email, no password. Rihla is ready the instant you open it.
• Made for the Gulf. Built around the Omani Rial and tracked to the baisa with full 3-decimal precision.
• English and Arabic. Fully localized with complete right-to-left support.

EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO SHARE EXPENSES

• Invite friends with a short 6-character code, a QR scan, or a link that drops them straight into the group.
• Tag expenses by category — food, lodging, transit, groceries, activities, and more — so you can see where the money went.
• Follow every expense and payment as it happens, with a clear activity log for each event, each group, and across all your groups.
• Turn on push notifications for new expenses and payments, and switch them off anytime.
• Optionally link an email to recover your groups and history if you switch or reset your device.
• Choose light or dark mode, or let Rihla follow your device.

PERFECT FOR

• Trips and holidays with friends
• Roommates splitting rent and shared bills
• Dinners and restaurant bills with the group
• Any group that shares costs again and again

Stop doing the math in your head. Rihla tracks shared expenses, tells you who owes who, and settles up with the fewest possible payments — so money never gets in the way of the people you share it with.
