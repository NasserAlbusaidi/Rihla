Rihla helps friend groups in Oman and the Gulf split bills, track shared expenses, and settle up clearly. Use it for Salalah trips, weekend plans, dinner bills, roommate costs, camping, groceries, and any moment where one person pays for the group.

No signup required. Open Rihla, create a group, invite friends with WhatsApp, and add the first real expense. Everyone can see who owes who and the simplest payments needed to settle up.

WHY RIHLA

• Built for recurring groups. Keep one group for your people, then add trips, dinners, and outings inside it.
• OMR and Gulf-friendly money tracking. Record shared expenses in supported currencies with precise decimal handling.
• WhatsApp-ready invites. Share a link, QR code, or 6-character invite code.
• Settle up with fewer payments. Rihla calculates the simplest transfers to clear balances.
• Flexible splitting. Split equally, by shares, by exact amounts, or by percentage.
• Works offline. Add expenses when the signal is weak; changes sync when you are back online.
• Arabic and English. Full right-to-left support for Arabic.

PERFECT FOR

• Trips and holidays with friends
• Dinner, coffee, and majlis bills
• Roommates splitting groceries, utilities, and rent extras
• Groups that share costs again and again

EVERYTHING YOU NEED

• Track expenses and payments by group and event.
• Categorize expenses like food, lodging, transit, groceries, and activities.
• See who owes who across a single event or the whole group.
• Turn push notifications on or off anytime.
• Optionally link an email to recover your groups and history if you switch or reset your device.
• Choose light mode, dark mode, or device theme.

Rihla means "journey." Start with your next shared bill.
