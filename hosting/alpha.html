If Play says unavailable

Most install failures are a Google account mismatch.

A

Check the active Play account

Google Play must be signed into the same account that was added to the tester list.

B

Open the opt-in link first

The public Play listing can look unavailable until the tester opt-in step is complete.

C

Return to the group invite

If you installed from a group invite and the code is not filled, tap the WhatsApp invite again after installing.
