Splitwise alternative for Oman

Built around the way groups here actually share costs.

Salalah and weekend trips

One person pays for the stay, another pays for fuel, someone else buys food. Rihla keeps the shared ledger clear.

Restaurant and majlis bills

Add the bill while everyone is still there, split evenly or custom, and settle without rebuilding the math in chat.

Roommates and shared housing

Keep one persistent group for groceries, utilities, rent extras, and supplies that repeat every month.

How it works

Create the group once. Use it every time money is shared.

1

2

3

Add expenses and settle up

Use it for the next real shared bill.
