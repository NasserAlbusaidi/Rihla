Delete your data

How to remove your data from Rihla — and what gets removed.

Rihla starts with an anonymous session created on your device. If you linked an email for recovery, that email is attached to the same Firebase user. Deletion is a two-part process: data that lives on your device, and data that lives on our backend.

This action is permanent. Deleted data cannot be restored. If you are part of a shared group, your group members will lose visibility into expenses you added once your records are scrubbed.

1. Delete local data (you can do this yourself)

Uninstalling the Rihla app removes everything stored locally on your device — the offline cache, your anonymous session, and the device name you saved in app settings. On Android: long-press the app icon → Uninstall. On iOS: long-press the app icon → Remove App → Delete App.

If you reinstall the app afterwards, you will start with a fresh anonymous session and will not see your previous groups unless you restore with a linked recovery email.

2. Delete server data (email request)

Because Rihla cannot identify you without your active device, the way to request deletion of data stored on our backend is to email us. Send a message to nasserbusaidi@gmail.com from any address with the following information:

  • Subject line: Rihla data deletion request
  • The display name(s) you used inside Rihla.
  • The invite code(s) of the group(s) you participated in, if you have them. (Open the group → Settings → Invite code.)
  • Any expense descriptions or dates that will help us locate your records. The more specific you are, the faster we can find them.

What we delete

  • Your linked recovery email, if one is attached to your Firebase user.
  • Your push-notification token from our backend.
  • Your membership in any groups you identify.
  • Your display name from those groups' participant lists. Existing expenses that reference your participant ID are scrubbed so other members no longer see your name attached to them.
  • Any receipt photos you uploaded.

What we keep

  • The bare existence of an expense in the group ledger may remain so the other members' balances stay correct — your name is removed, but the amount that was split is not retroactively erased from their history.
  • Crash and error reports captured by Sentry. These do not contain your display name or group data and are deleted on Sentry's default retention schedule.

How long this takes

Deletion requests are processed manually. Please allow up to 30 days. We will reply to the address you wrote in from once the deletion is complete.
