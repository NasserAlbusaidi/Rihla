#351 · Records the payment — it doesn't move money

The Mark-Paid sheet logs a settlement in the ledger; no bank transfer happens. People expect Mark paid to move real money. Make it explicit on the sheet, and split the success confirmation so a queued offline write reads Saved offline — will sync rather than a false "recorded". This is a copy/states fix, not a visual fork — the two phones show the sheet and the online vs offline toasts, not competing designs. ← gallery

design(settle-up): "Rihla records, doesn't move money" microcopy + offline-saved toast

P3 · design · l10n · record_payment_sheet.dart

The sheet already carries a saffron banner — "This records your payment to Khalid immediately." It says when but not what: that recording ≠ transferring. Add one plain line under it ("Rihla records the payment — it doesn't move money"), and branch the success confirmation on connectivity so a locally-queued write is honest about not yet being synced.

Recommendation: Ship both states — the microcopy line and the connectivity-branched toast. They solve the same "did money actually move?" doubt at two moments (before confirm, and after).
