#285 · Don't let a reinstall wipe their groups

Identity is a device-bound anonymous UID — a reinstall, a new phone, or "clear data" silently orphans every group and expense unless an email was linked beforehand. Today the only hint is a buried Linked email · Not set row in Profile, and active users see no prompt at all. We add a one-time, non-blocking nudge after the first group/expense — copy that states the real stakes without alarming. Hits joiners hardest (they just typed a code; zero account mental model).
