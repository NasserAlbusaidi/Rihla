Interaction spec (single source of truth)

  1. One "Split" card replaces the three _Sections (Paid by / Split between / How). Category strip stays above it; "Where" (event · date) stays as its own section below.
  2. Paid by — one control. Inline row at the top of the card: avatar + name + a "Change" affordance, tapping anywhere on the row opens the existing _PayerPickerSheet (#280). Delete the second payer picker — remove _PayerSelector from SplitScopeSelector and stop threading payerId through the customise flow.
  3. Who splits — scope segmented control in plain language: Everyone (global) · Some people (custom) · Just me (personal). Selecting "Some people" reveals the existing _CustomParticipantSelector roster inline; the other two hide it. (New l10n values for the three labels — the editorScope* keys today read "Everyone / Custom / Personal".)
  4. How — mode segmented control: Equal · Shares · Exact · %. Equal needs no further input. Shares / Exact / % open the existing showCustomSplitSheet for the weights — its returned distribution flows straight into the rows. Mode is reflected in the segmented control. (Itemized stays reachable inside that sheet, displayed as Exact.)
  5. Per-person rows reuse _SplitPreviewCard's allocateExpenseOwed figures verbatim (#242) — real owed per person, the payer marked, your own row highlighted. Rendered as a vertical list (not the horizontal strip) so names + amounts read top-to-bottom.
  6. Reconciliation footer: "✓ Adds up to {total}" — sum of the rendered shares, which by construction equals the amount.
  7. Sub-two-people guard unchanged: with <2 eligible people the rows collapse to the "pick at least two people to split" hint and the mode control is disabled (same as today's _SplitModeCard disabled state).
Gate: IA/layout + copy only. No BalanceCalculator / firestore.rules / routing / schema change — the per-person numbers (#242) and the weights sheet are reused untouched. Gate-exempt.
