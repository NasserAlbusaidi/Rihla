design(groups): polish the creator-side "Claim requests" / Approve card (#278 A6)

P3 · design · widget: ClaimRequestsSection · GroupSettingsScreen

All variants live inside claim_requests_section.dart and change nothing about decideClaimRequest. They reuse the existing people-row vocabulary (RAvatar, the dashed shadow avatar, pill-tag, the raised card) so the card stops looking bolted-on. The shared goals: give the requester a face, make the shadow → joiner merge legible at a glance, signal these rows are pending decisions, and state the irreversible balance inheritance in copy (mirroring the joiner-side warning).

Recommendation: Variant 2 (identity-merge card) — the dual-avatar "Basma → Sara's spot" visual plus the one-line consequence is what turns a functional affordance into a deliberate, money-aware surface. Claim requests are rare (1–2 at a time), so the extra height is affordable and the explicitness is worth it for an irreversible action.
