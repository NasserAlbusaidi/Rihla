Why Rihla

Built for how the GCC actually splits

  1. One crew, many trips

    Persistent groups hold every dinner, weekend, and shared bill inside them — no starting over each time, and balances carry across events.

  2. The fewest transfers

    Settlement optimization works out the smallest set of payments to make everyone even — fewer “send me half and I’ll send you the rest” messages.

  3. Arabic & English, properly

    Full right-to-left support — not a bolted-on translation. Switch languages and the whole app mirrors the way it should.

  4. Real Gulf money

    OMR with three decimals done right, plus AED, SAR, QAR, USD, EUR and GBP — each group keeps its own currency.

  5. Works with no signal

    Add expenses in the desert, on the plane, anywhere — Rihla saves them offline and syncs the moment you’re back online.
