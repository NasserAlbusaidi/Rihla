design · privacy · Gate-exempt · #708 Settle & Seal (sequenced first)

The flow is unchanged up to the existing Mark paid sheet. The settlement records immediately (append-only, as today). Then, only when the viewer is the debtor (currentUid == fromUserId), a small post-record nudge offers to let the creditor know. The creditor-records (#282) and settle-on-behalf (#595) perspectives show no nudge — only the person who actually paid informs. Copy is honest past tense because it fires after recording.

Recommendation: Variant A — a follow-up nudge sheet. It matches the handoff spec's two-button [Not now] [WhatsApp] shape, and crucially it shows the exact message before it sends (no surprise about what gets shared). The snackbar (B) can't preview the message and inherits the action-snackbar auto-dismiss trap (#411).

Message scope & rules
Event settle →  for {event} in {group} Group settle →  for {group}
  • Past tense, courtesy only: “Hey {name}, I’ve sent you {amount} for {scope}.” — fires after recording, never a payment step.
  • Amount = the live/edited amount in the sheet (partial pays carry the edited figure), per-currency decimals incl. JPY×1. Amount stays LTR-Latin inside Arabic.
  • Debtor-only: shown when currentUid == fromUserId (paying). Creditor-records (#282) & settle-on-behalf (#595) → no nudge.
  • Scope naming disambiguates the same event-name across two shared groups; group settle spans events so it never names one event.
  • Single-tile path only for v1 — the stepped multi-currency walk shows its own aggregate snackbar; per-step WhatsApp nudges would be noisy and the message carries one amount/currency.
  • Fallback: WhatsApp not installed → shareText OS share sheet (never a dead end).
