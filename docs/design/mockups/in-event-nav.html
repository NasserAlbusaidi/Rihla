Inside an event — one surface, not four pages

Today the EventCommandCenter hub is a launchpad: the full ledger, the activity timeline, and settle-up are each a separate page you drill into. Two directions to collapse that. Tap the tabs in A and the balance bar in B — they’re live.

A — Tabs: familiar, safe; every facet becomes a tab switch, zero page pushes.  ·  B — Ledger-first: the boldest “it’s just a bill splitter” shape — the event is the expense list; balance/settle pulls down, activity is a filter. Pick the feel; we mock the winner to spec.
