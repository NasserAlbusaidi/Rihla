#359 · Shareable settlement receipt

When a payment is recorded on Settle Up, both sides want a keepable proof — for the WhatsApp tracker that doesn't move money. The share itself is plain text ("Ahmed paid Sara OMR 5.000 · cash · Jun 7") sent through the iOS-safe shareText() helper. The fork is the in-app affordance that sits beside the payment-history tile (settle_up_page_body.dart:382). Each phone shows the success/share surface plus the resulting share-text block it produces. ← gallery

#359

design(settle-up): shareable settlement receipt card via shareText()

P3 · design · reuses #291/#308 shareText

All three produce the same plain-text receipt — the difference is how much ceremony the in-app confirmation carries. A perforated .ticket stub frames the payment as a physical proof (on-brand journey artifact); the others are progressively plainer. None changes the money model: a settlement stays an append-only row, and the share is read-only text — nothing writes on tap.

Recommendation: Variant V1 (ticket stub) — the perforated card already carries Rihla's journey identity, so a recorded payment feels like a stub you tore off and can hand over.
