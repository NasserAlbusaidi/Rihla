Rihla رحلة

Falaj فَلَج

One flow, split fairly. · ساقية واحدة، قسمة عادلة

A new identity and lower-friction structure for Rihla, drawn from the thing Oman actually invented for fair splitting: the UNESCO-listed aflaj — one water channel divided into measured shares so every household gets exactly what it is owed. That is this product's mechanic, from home ground instead of a borrowed editorial recipe. This page is set in the proposed system itself — plaster & brass by day, kohl & lantern by night (toggle your theme), Bricolage Grotesque + Zain + Spline Sans Mono throughout.

01 · Diagnosis

Why the current design reads “Claude”

It isn't one token — it's that all three legs of Anthropic's own recipe are present at once: a warm ivory ground, a terracotta-family accent, and an editorial serif doing the display voice. Each alone is defensible; together they are the Claude aesthetic family.

The identity-forensics pass confirmed the resemblance is combinatorial: warm paper (both R≫B warm whites), a burnt-orange accent within a few degrees of hue, serif-italic display, paper-grain texture, sepia hairlines. Meanwhile the genuinely Rihla-owned assets — the perforated ticket cards, the trip-stamp ritual, mono tabular money, the sage/rust polarity contract, the quiet “everyone's even” voice — have nothing to do with Claude at all. The fix: break all three legs at once, keep everything that was already ours.

Market scan: every splitter is flat friendly SaaS — Splitwise mint, Tricount petrol, Settle Up orange, Splid turquoise, Spliit stock-shadcn. Nobody owns a travel narrative, nobody owns a real dark theme, nobody owns the Gulf. The whitespace is exactly where Rihla already lives.

02 · Friction audit

Where the taps go today

Eight core jobs were tap-counted against the live router and screens. The structure is mostly sound — the balance question is already answered above the fold — but the most frequent job in the app pays a picker sheet it shouldn't, and one hierarchy level taxes the common case.

The five structural fixes

  • One-tap FAB. The fast path exists but is dead for every multi-group user — a self-inflicted wound: create-group auto-seeds an open event per group, so the “sole open event” condition never holds. Default to the ranked top-priority event (the ranking already exists), demote the sheet to an escape hatch.
  • Smart-forward group rows. A group with exactly one open event skips the group screen and lands in the event workspace; the group overview stays one tap away in the header. Multi-event groups keep the overview. URLs unchanged.
  • Honest hero. The home balance card advertises the money answer but taps to a scroll animation. Make it open a per-group breakdown whose rows deep-link to settle-up (?memberId= preselect already supported).
  • One chrome per surface. Every event module exists twice — as a workspace tab and a standalone full-chrome route. Keep the URLs as thin shims that open the workspace on the right tab (tab from the path segment); delete the duplicate chrome.
  • Global search. Search today is event-scoped only; finding an old expense requires remembering group AND event. Add top-level /search over groups, events, expenses.

04 · The Falaj system

Materials: plaster, shale, brass

Surfaces are Muscat gypsum-plaster white — a cool mineral white (G ≥ R; Anthropic ivory is yellow-warm). Ink is Hajar-shale near-black with a faint green-gray cast. The single accent is burnished brass — mandoos studs, dhow fittings, astrolabes — a golden-olive a full hue family away from terracotta. Money speaks in its own two voices, disjoint from the brand accent: date-palm emerald and pomegranate crimson.

The brand device: the falaj fork

A single rule that branches into shares — wordmark underscore, settle-up transfer connector, section tick, and sync indicator. Usage law (to prevent dilution): the full fork appears at most once per screen; the share-notch tick is the only ambient form. Continuous “water flow” motion is reserved exclusively for the syncing state — if something flows, the app is syncing, nothing else ever animates continuously.

The flow, as the offline→sync indicator.

Signature moves

  • Manifest tickets, re-dressed. The perforated ticket silhouette stays (it's the most Rihla-owned asset); materials change to plaster, shale ink, one brass tear-line rule, a brass corner seal — and CoverArt bands repaint to five Omani landscape palettes (Hajar dawn, Wadi pool, Khareef, Sharqiya night, Muscat harbor).
  • Brass port-seal stamps. The 12 trip-stamp glyphs redraw as engraved circular port-registry seals. Settling a debt lands a physical stamp: SETTLED · تمّت التسوية — the app's one celebratory flourish, with Jawaz's grafted stamp physics (300ms scale 1.08→1.0, −3°→0°, one-frame ink bloom).
  • Night-navigation dark. Kohl ground, lantern brass, faint star-grid on hero surfaces (≤3% opacity, tokenized). No competitor has a designed dark theme; this one has a story.
  • Bilingual Kufi-grid lockup. “Rihla” and “رحلة” drawn on one square grid at equal cap height, fork-underscore mirroring with reading direction. The fork alone, as a brass seal on kohl, is the app icon — legible at 16px.
  • Polarity carets. Grafted from Kamal: every hero money readout carries a ▲/▼ share-notch caret beside the color — owed/owe is never color-alone (colorblind-safe).
  • Document-close ritual. Grafted from Jawaz: every artifact that leaves the app (receipts, share cards) ends with the fork underscore + “recorded in Rihla”.

06 · Decisions & guardrails

What breaks, what stays, what got fixed in synthesis

KEEP-list decisions (explicit, per the contract)

InvariantDecisionRationale
KEEP-1 · type system (serif italic + mono + sans)Break facesThe italic serif is the sharpest Claude signal and has no Arabic form. The three-tier structure (display / mono money / sans UI) and the #841 per-script engine survive intact.
KEEP-2 · warm cream palette + sage/rustBreak huesCream is the resemblance. The structural money contract survives exactly: surface + darker AA text per polarity, disjoint from brand accent, green=owed prior kept.
KEEP-3 · dashed-arrow transfer cardsKeepRestyled — the connector becomes the falaj fork; who-pays-whom clarity unchanged.
KEEP-4 · RTL buildKeep + strengthenZain is Arabic-first; the lockup is bilingual by construction; the fork mirrors natively.
KEEP-5 · copy voiceKeep verbatimThe voice was never the problem — “everyone's even” finally gets a visual system as quiet as it is.
KEEP-6 · perforated ticket cardsKeep silhouetteThe most Rihla-owned asset. Materials re-dress: plaster, shale, brass seal, Omani landscape covers.
KEEP-7 · “saved · synced” modalKeepStructural trust pattern; restyle only.
KEEP-8 · light card hierarchyBreak valuesTwo-level page-vs-card pattern kept on the new plaster/white stack.
DEC-3 · deterministic avatarsKeep contractSame hash, same 8 slots — retinted as a set to the seal-ink palette.

Judge-flagged issues, fixed before you're reading this

  • Brass was AA-knife-edge. Authored primary #96660F gave white 4.99:1. Fixed: light primary darkens to #8A5D0D (5.75:1, margin for pressed states); #96660F survives only as a decorative bright variant. Dark mode already ran ink-on-brass at 7.6:1. Add an automated contrast test over token pairs to CI so drift can't re-break it.
  • Warning collided with brass. Amber #F59E0B sits in the gold family the brand now owns — an offline banner reading as “brand accent” damages the offline-honesty trust pattern. Fixed: warning re-hues to burnt orange-red (#C2410C light / #E8703A dark), banners become icon-led, and brass + warning are forbidden in the same component.
  • The last cream echo. The authored selection tints (#E9DFC2/#F3EDDB) were the one bridge back to the saffron look. Fixed: cooled to #E3E4C9/#F1F2DF, with two written rules — the page ground must stay G≥R, and a tint is never the only selection signal (always + hairline + tick).
  • Fork dilution risk. Usage law written into the spec: full fork max once per screen; the share-notch tick is the only ambient form; continuous flow-motion means “syncing” and nothing else. Test the app icon at 16px against the git-fork association before committing the lockup.

The one open bet: Zain's Latin

Every judge flagged it: Zain is superb Arabic and characterful Latin, but it ships no 500/600 weights (two-step 400/700 ladder — ~215 w500/w600 call sites would remap) and its rounded Latin is unproven at dense 13sp ledger rows. Decision path: this page is spike #1; spike #2 is one Flutter screen (ledger + hero + settings) in both scripts before the font PR. Pre-agreed fallback, so it's a decision and not a scramble: IBM Plex Sans takes Latin UI, Zain keeps Arabic + display. Either way the identity holds — the palette, fork, and structure carry it.
