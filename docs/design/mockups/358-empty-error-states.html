#358 · Empty & error states

Three gaps in how Rihla recovers from emptiness and failure. (1) When Firestore denies a group read — you were removed — there's no human-facing state; the raw permission-denied just dead-ends (group_provider.dart:250). (2) The category-filtered ledger empty state (ledger_screen.dart:297) tells you "nothing here" but offers no way out. (3) The orphaned NetworkErrorWidget (error_widgets.dart:14, with .offline()/.loadingError() + Retry) is unused while screens hand-roll error views — adopt it. These are a states gallery, not competing layouts — except the network error, which gets two real treatments. ← gallery

#358

design(empty-states): removed-from-group · clear-filters CTA · adopt NetworkErrorWidget

P2 · design · raw error hidden, logged to Sentry

States 1–2 are single correct designs (no fork) — the removed-from-group page and the clear-filters CTA. State 3 is a genuine fork: the same NetworkErrorWidget can land as a friendly full-bleed empty-state or a compact inline retry strip, depending on how much of the screen the failure owns. Both reuse the widget's onRetry and its .offline() copy.

Recommendation: Friendly full-bleed for whole-screen failures (the ledger/group can't load at all) — it reads as a designed state, not a crash. Keep the compact strip for partial failures inside an otherwise-loaded screen.
