Add expense — make every split option clear & visible

Reported friction: “it took me a while to find the itemized tab.” The audit confirms why — itemized is buried two levels deep. The Split card's How row shows only 4 modes (split_card.dart:452-463); Itemized exists only as a 5th ellipsis-cramped chip inside a bottom sheet that opens after you tap an unrelated mode (custom_split_sheet_mode_selector.dart:37-53). Reaching it takes 2 non-obvious taps + a modality switch — and the default (Equally) never opens the sheet, so it's discovered by accident. Worked example: Dinner at Bait Al Luban · 4 people · OMR 42.500. ← gallery

FIX

Give the split a clear front door

every mode gets an icon + words · Itemized becomes a first-class option

Three directions, same worked example. All promote Itemized to a peer of the other modes, add an icon + a plain-language label so nothing is “just another split-math variant,” and fix the cramped/ellipsized row. They differ on the visible-at-rest ↔ clarity ↔ 90%-speed trade-off.

Recommendation: A. It answers the literal ask — every option visible at rest, no hunting — while icons + a contextual helper line make each one clear, and a 2-line wrap kills the ellipsis/RTL truncation. Reach for C if you also want to delete the modality switch (no sheet at all) and keep the common Equally case one-glance simple.
