#365

feat(home): per-person net across all groups (Splitwise “Friends” view)

P2 · design · decision · cluster:multi-currency · blocked by #261

All three sit beneath the existing hero + two subtotals on the home dashboard (balance_hero_card.dart stays untouched; this is a new section). They differ on how the same per-person data is grouped and drilled into. Net per person is the signed sum of that person's balance across every shared group, keyed by stable userId — sage = they owe you, rust = you owe them. Currency caveat: amounts are OMR-only until #261 lands a real aggregation contract; a mixed-currency person is blocked, not summed (10 USD + 10 OMR ≠ “20”).

Recommendation: Variant V1 (flat net list) — one tap-through row per person, lowest friction, mirrors Splitwise Friends and the existing group-row pattern in home_screen.dart.
