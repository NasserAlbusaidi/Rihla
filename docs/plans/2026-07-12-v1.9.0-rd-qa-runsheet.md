**Prepared:** 2026-07-12 · **Release:** v1.9.0 (versionCode 36) · **Backend:** prod `rihla-safar`
**Tracks release blocker #40.** Formal gate cells are RD-01..RD-13 (`docs/REAL-DEVICE-QA.md`).

## Devices

| Tag | Device | Account | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Pixel 9 Pro XL (Android 16) | founder's REAL linked account | throwaway groups only — **NO account delete / sign-out swap / restore / conflict** |
| **B** | Samsung SM-G770F (Android 13) | all guest/anon | every destructive account-state test happens here |

`[Both]` = cross-device (A owns the shared group, B is the second client, or vice-versa where noted).
`[edge]` marks edge/boundary cases. Destructive cases are ordered LAST within each block; **account-destructive** (delete / sign-out / swap) run strictly on Device B at the very end of the sheet.
